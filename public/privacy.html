Privacy & ethics notice

Student consent required: before a class can monitor your activity, you need to read this and decide. There is no account and no password: you are identified by your email and class code.

What is collected, and only while you are in a class

  • Browser extension (Google Meet / Zoom web client only): a focus score (0–100), how long since you last used the mouse or keyboard in the meeting, how often you were active in the last minute (counts only, never which keys), whether you are scrolling or a video/audio is playing in the meeting tab, how many times you left the meeting for another tab or app, and how many chat messages you sent in the meeting. A badge is shown on the meeting page whenever it is on.
  • Your dashboard (this website, optional): it only shows you your own live numbers. It does not watch your mouse, keyboard or tabs.
  • Your details: your name, your email, the class you joined, and this consent decision with its date and version. There is no password: students do not have one.

Never collected: passwords, what you type (including your meeting chat text and the text of your replies to educator questions, which are not stored), screenshots, camera, microphone, files, or your browsing history. The extension cannot see the address of tabs other than Meet/Zoom and this app.

Who sees it

  • The educator who owns the class sees your name and the indicators above, live, and can export the current table as a CSV file to their own computer.
  • You see exactly what they see about you, live, on your dashboard. Other students never see your data; they only see the class average.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. This app loads no external scripts, fonts or trackers.

How long it is kept

  • Live activity is held in the server’s memory only (never written to disk). It is forgotten about an hour after you disconnect, when the server restarts, when your educator clears the class data, or immediately if you withdraw consent, leave the class or delete your account.
  • Your name, email, class membership and consent record are kept until you delete your data.

Fairness: these are signals, not proof

Indicators can be wrong. A student may be listening, reading, taking notes on paper, using assistive technology, or having connection problems. Educators are asked to treat a flag as a reason to check in kindly, never as proof of inattention or as the sole basis for a grade or disciplinary decision.

Your controls

  • Withdraw consent at any time on this page: monitoring stops at once and your live data is deleted.
  • Leave a class from your dashboard.
  • Delete your data from your dashboard.
  • Saying no is fine: you simply cannot join a monitored class. Ask your educator about an alternative way to take part.
